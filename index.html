About Me

I'm a rising senior at Northwestern University pursuing a combined BS/MS in Mechanical Engineering and Electrical Engineering. I'm trilingual in English, Spanish, and Korean, and previously served alongside the U.S. Army's 2nd Infantry Division as a Korean Augmentation to the United States Army (KATUSA) soldier.

My journey as an engineer is driven by a passion for combining technology and social impact, designing systems that are both innovative and meaningful to people's lives. I bring my international background and passion for engineering to the way I approach complex, real-world problems.

With strong interests in robotics, mechanical engineering research, medical devices, manufacturing, and entrepreneurship, I want to explore and test the limits of human and technical capabilities through engineering and its practical applications.
